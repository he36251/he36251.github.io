Glosa: Offline AI Translator

Translate text, speech and photos with an AI model that runs on your phone. Download the model once and Glosa works offline, even in airplane mode. Your translations never leave your phone.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Translate text in 190+ languages, with automatic language detection
  • Speak instead of typing, and hear the translation read aloud
  • Point your camera at a menu or sign to see the translation on top
  • Translate text in photos from your gallery
  • Choose a tone, and tap Explain to see why a translation reads the way it does
  • Searchable history, stored only on your device

Privacy

No account needed. Translation runs entirely on your device: your text, speech, photos and history are never uploaded.

Glosa is free and supported by non-personalized ads from Google AdMob. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) include your device model and OS version, never your content.

Before you install

  • Requires a 64-bit ARM phone
  • The AI model is a large download (586 MB to 3.7 GB), so use Wi-Fi
